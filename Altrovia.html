Altrovia AI

Altrovia AI was hard to understand at a glance. We reshaped the experience to lead with clear value, simplified task flow, and concrete visuals so researchers can quickly see how the product helps plan and analyze clinical trials.

Role Lead Designer
Team Karen, Olivia, Jane
Timeline 2 days
Context Designathon

Problem

Altrovia wasn't clear about its value.

The original site didn't clearly communicate Altrovia AI’s value or how it fits into a researcher’s workflow. Users needed faster access to the product’s core capabilities and practical use cases.

The redesign made visitors immediately understand how Altrovia supports trial planning and analysis.

How it came together.

01

Start with wireframes and references.

We first explored low-fidelity wireframes alongside competitive references from healthcare and SaaS products. This helped us identify effective design patterns and uncover features our product was missing.

As the designathon lead, I organized the project timeline, coordinated team meetings, and guided our design process by leading collaborative critiques that combined the strongest ideas from each wireframe into a unified direction.

The goal was to communicate Altrovia's value within the first few seconds of landing on the page. The selected direction prioritized visual examples over dense text, making the product easier to understand at a glance.

02

Refine, simplify and repeat.

Due to the designathon's time constraints, we quickly refined our strongest wireframes instead of exploring entirely new concepts. We adopted a clean, modern visual style inspired by fintech products, using a blue color palette to reinforce trust and credibility. Rather than incorporating flashy animations, we prioritized a simple, distraction-free interface that helped users finish their tasks efficiently. This approach aligned with the needs of our target audience, who value clarity and speed over visual effects, but also embodying trustworthiness that pharmaceutical companies are known for.

03

Combine strengths and polish the final product.

Finally, we combined the strongest ideas from each concept, polished the designs, and delivered a final prototype that makes clinical trial planning easier to understand and easier to engage with.

What I Learned

Key takeaways from the project.

  • Working under tight time constraints required clear tradeoffs and fast decision-making.
  • Rapid iteration helped uncover usability gaps before the final launch.
  • Collaborating in a hackathon taught me how to align ideas quickly and decisively.
  • This project shaped a more efficient, research-informed design process.
